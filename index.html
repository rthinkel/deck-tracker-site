About

DeckTracker is a music tracker designed for the Steam Deck. It uses a pattern-based tracker interface for sequencing notes and arranging songs, with controller-oriented navigation and editing.

Features

  • Multiple instrument engines: analog, wavetable, 808, 4-operator FM, sampler, and SoundFont support
  • Multiple effects for instruments, channels, and mastering: parametric EQ, compressor with sidechaining, limiter, delay, reverb, distortion, chorus, and phaser
  • Full WAV export: master mix or individual channels
  • Instrument and effect preset functionality
  • Stereo mixer with master metering and output visualization
  • Controller-native Steam Deck workflow
  • Dozens of themes
